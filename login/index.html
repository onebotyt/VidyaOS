Install VidyaOS on iOS

Apple iOS runs VidyaOS as a standalone Progressive Web App with full-screen native feel and hardware acceleration.

1
Open in Safari Ensure you are browsing VidyaOS on Apple Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2

3
Select "Add to Home Screen" Scroll down the action sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap "Add" Tap Add in the top-right corner. VidyaOS will now launch full-screen from your Home Screen!
