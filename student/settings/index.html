UBTER Attendance Guidelines

1. Statutory 75% Requirement: Every enrolled student must maintain a minimum of 75.0% aggregate attendance across theory and lab sessions to be eligible for board examinations.

2. Medical Concessions: Students with authorized medical leaves approved through the portal may be granted up to 10% relaxation as per university norms.

3. Biometric Verification: Real-time ESP32 optical biometric logs and digital faculty roll calls feed directly into institutional compliance records.
